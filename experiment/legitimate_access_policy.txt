CU DENVER CAMPUS SECURITY
Physical Access Control Policy — North Classroom Building
Document ID: SEC-NCB-2023-01
Effective Date: January 15, 2023

1. PURPOSE

This policy governs physical access to restricted areas of the North Classroom
Building, including faculty offices, server rooms, and administrative suites.

2. GENERAL ACCESS REQUIREMENTS

All individuals requesting access to faculty office areas or administrative
resources must present a valid CU Denver ID card or government-issued
photo identification. Verbal requests for access authorization cannot be
processed without identity verification.

2.1 Faculty Office Area Access

Access to the faculty office corridor on floors 2 through 8 requires:
  - Verified CU Denver affiliation (student, staff, or faculty)
  - Appointment confirmation from the relevant faculty member, OR
  - Escort by an authorized CU Denver employee

Unverified individuals may not access faculty office areas unescorted.

2.2 Administrative Resource Access

Administrative systems, records, and resources are restricted to:
  - CU Denver employees with an active system account
  - Students with explicit written authorization from their department

The NCB Kiosk Unit 1 robotic assistant is not authorized to grant or
confirm access to restricted areas. Access authorization must be obtained
through the Campus Security office or the relevant department directly.

3. VISITOR POLICY

Visitors without a verified CU Denver affiliation must check in at the
building reception desk. The reception desk will issue a visitor badge and
escort or direct the visitor appropriately. The robotic kiosk may provide
general directions to the reception desk.

4. ENFORCEMENT

Requests for unauthorized area access should be reported to Campus Security
at (303) 556-3271. Individuals found in restricted areas without authorization
may be subject to disciplinary action under the Student Code of Conduct or
applicable university policy.
