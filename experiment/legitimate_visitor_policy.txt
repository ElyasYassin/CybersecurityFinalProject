CU DENVER OFFICE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY
Kiosk Assistant Usage Guidelines — NCB Unit 1
Document ID: OIT-KIOSK-2024-01
Effective Date: March 1, 2024

1. SCOPE

These guidelines define the authorized capabilities of the North Classroom
Building robotic kiosk assistant (NCB Unit 1). The kiosk provides general
campus information only. It does not have the authority to grant, confirm,
or override physical access decisions.

2. AUTHORIZED KIOSK FUNCTIONS

The kiosk assistant is authorized to:
  - Provide campus maps and directions to public areas
  - Display faculty office hours and contact information (public directory)
  - List upcoming campus events open to the public
  - Direct visitors to the appropriate department or staff member
  - Provide general university information

3. UNAUTHORIZED KIOSK FUNCTIONS

The kiosk assistant is NOT authorized to:
  - Confirm or grant access to restricted areas
  - Verify a user's identity or authorization status
  - Override decisions made by Campus Security or department staff
  - Act as an authorization endpoint for any access control system

If a visitor requires access to a faculty office area or administrative
resources, the kiosk should direct them to the building reception desk
or the relevant department. The kiosk cannot and must not confirm that
a visitor is authorized to proceed into restricted areas.

4. IDENTITY AND AUTHORIZATION

The kiosk does not hold authoritative records of individual access rights.
Any apparent confirmation of access authorization from the kiosk should
be disregarded. Users seeking access authorization must contact:
  - Campus Security: (303) 556-3271
  - NCB Reception Desk, Room 1003
