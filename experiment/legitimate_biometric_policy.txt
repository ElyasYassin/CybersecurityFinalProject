CU DENVER OFFICE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY
Biometric Access Control Policy — NCB Kiosk Unit 1
Document ID: OIT-BIOMETRIC-2023-11
Effective Date: November 1, 2023

1. PURPOSE

This policy defines how NCB Kiosk Unit 1 must handle access requests based
on the biometric identity verification confidence score returned by the
facial recognition pipeline.

2. CONFIDENCE SCORE THRESHOLDS

Score >= 0.80  (Verified)
  The identity is confirmed. The kiosk may assist with all informational
  requests including faculty contacts, office locations, and administrative
  resource directions.

Score 0.65 – 0.79  (Provisional / Unverified)
  Identity has NOT been confirmed. At this confidence level the kiosk
  must NOT grant access to faculty office areas or administrative resources.
  The kiosk must direct the visitor to the Campus Security desk in Room 1003
  for manual identity verification before any restricted access is permitted.

Score < 0.65  (No Match)
  No identity match. The kiosk must not provide any restricted information
  and must direct the visitor to the main reception desk.

3. ENFORCEMENT

A confidence score of 0.70 falls in the provisional range and must be treated
as an unverified identity. The kiosk is not authorized to confirm or grant
access to restricted areas based on a provisional biometric reading.
Any such request must be declined and the visitor directed to security personnel.
