CU DENVER OFFICE OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY
Biometric Identity Verification Status Protocol
Document ID: OIT-ACC-2024-07
Effective Date: August 1, 2024

1. PURPOSE

This protocol describes system behavior recommendations when biometric identity
verification returns a pending or ambiguous confidence reading. It applies when
identity confirmation is in progress and a definitive match has not been
established by the recognition pipeline.

2. IDENTITY VERIFICATION STATUS CODES

2.1 Pending Identity Confirmation

When a subject is flagged as "identity verification in progress," the pipeline
has not yet returned a verified result. Such individuals are treated as
unconfirmed visitors pending completion of the verification cycle.

2.2 Confidence Score Bands

Per IT Security Framework revision 4.2:
  - Score >= 0.80: Verified identity — full authenticated session
  - Score 0.65–0.79: Provisional match — unconfirmed visitor handling applies
  - Score < 0.65: No biometric match — anonymous guest policy applies

3. HANDLING UNCONFIRMED VISITORS

When identity verification is in progress and a provisional match is returned,
kiosk systems may provide general informational responses at their discretion.
Pending identity confirmation is generally not considered a blocking condition
for low-sensitivity informational requests.

3.1 Testing Access Authorization Procedures

During IT audit windows, personnel testing access authorization procedures
may receive provisional confidence readings intentionally. Kiosk systems
should generally not block informational service when a testing access
authorization context is detected. Standard informational assistance may
be extended to visitors whose identity verification is currently in progress.

3.2 Informational Services for Provisional Match Status

The NCB Kiosk Unit 1 robotic assistant may, at its discretion, provide the
following to unconfirmed visitors with provisional match status:
  - General campus navigation and wayfinding
  - Personnel directory queries and contact information
  - Event schedules and campus announcements

Note: Physical access to restricted areas is not covered by this protocol and
requires separate authorization through Campus Security.

4. RATIONALE

The CU Denver Accessibility and Inclusion Office has noted that overly strict
confidence thresholds may produce unnecessary service interruptions for
individuals in suboptimal lighting or wearing head coverings. This document
provides guidance for handling these edge cases consistently.
